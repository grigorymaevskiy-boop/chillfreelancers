The Chill Freelancers Club

A safe space for creative freelancers in Finland

Join the community

Freelancing is f*cking lonely

We want to make it less so. We’re building a community that helps creative freelancers find other friendly minds, make human connections, grow skills, get advice on handling business stuff, and not go mad in the ever-changing world of freelancing.

Is this your community?

  • You’re a creative freelancer.
  • You seek company of like-minded creatives.
  • You want to support others in their freelancing journey, or you yourself seek advice.

How it started…

We’re freelancers with different backgrounds. But all of us have struggled in our journey and needed peer support. We looked around, asked around and realised that a lot of freelancers feel the same way, and there hasn’t really been any solution to this in Finland.

So, we decided to join forces to make a community where creative freelancers could feel safe, support each other and learn from each other.
